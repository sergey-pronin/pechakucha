# PechaKucha 15×15 — Read-Aloud Script

Each block = 15 seconds. Read continuously; the deck advances on its own.
Total runtime ≈ 3 minutes 45 seconds.

### 01 · COLD OPEN · 00:00

Soviet Ukraine, age ten. I found my older brother's Playboy in his drawer. I never looked at the women — I read the article about hackers stealing free pizza from Pizza Hut, and a man named Kevin Mitnick. That kid is your Head of Cybersecurity now. Hi.

### 02 · CHERKASY · 90s · 00:15

After that magazine: 90s Cherkasy. cDc, L0pht, PoC or GTFO. BMX, graffiti, streetball with the local crew. A hardware shop on the side, building PCs and fixing modems. Mr. Robot's Elliot's father, post-Soviet edition.

### 03 · KYIV · TELCO · 00:30

Then Kyiv. Volia, the country's largest telco. The moment "a network" stopped meaning four PCs in a back room and started meaning a country with internet.

### 04 · DISTRIBUTION · EE · 00:45

Next: technical lead across Eastern Europe for a security distributor. The role wasn't selling — it was consultancy with a demo. Customers called to understand whether Imperva or Qualys solved their problem. I'd show them, live, on their infrastructure. The product sold itself afterwards.

### 05 · BUG BOUNTY · 2015 · 01:00

2015, before bug bounty was mainstream. I tested Qualys' web scanner against Tesla and Western Union programs to understand what the outputs actually meant. Top 500 researcher on Bugcrowd. Never cashed a check. It was the most expensive education I never paid for.

### 06 · THE LEAP · MAY 2016 · 01:15

May 2016. A Polish infosec firm noticed me on Bugcrowd and made the offer. Pregnant wife. Three-year-old. No plan B. We moved. Six months in I founded DC4822, Warsaw's DEFCON chapter. Passed the torch since.

### 07 · THE TRIAL · 01:30

Instapage: scaled security at hypergrowth, four time zones, high eight-digit exit. Then Affirm — first security hire in Europe under Max Levchin. Two years of midnight finishes there. I caught the PayPal-mafia worldview and came back with a different center.

### 08 · OPERATING SYSTEM · 01:45

At Affirm I caught the Amazon disease — PR/FAQ before product, RFC before opinion, doc before meeting. I brought it here. If you've wondered why my Slack threads become Google Docs by Tuesday, that's why. Documents over decks. Only advance.

### 09 · NOW · BOOKSY · ET · YOU · 02:00

Which lands us here. I run cybersecurity at Booksy. Most of my hours are not in a terminal — they're in a room. With you. With engineering. With finance. I run security like a product function — with a roadmap, not a binder.

### 10 · HOW I LEAD · 02:15

How I lead. INTJ Architect — strategy and systems, low on small talk. My rule on team: your career is yours, not mine. I advise when asked. I unblock when you're stuck. I make myself replaceable on purpose. We ship awesome work. We are weirdos. Both are features.

### 11 · OFFSITE MOTTO · 02:30

This week's motto — the reason we're all in this room: we are DevSecOps. We want automation. We're not here to break things. We're here to use AI to empower mature engineering. Later I'll show you how I run this on myself, every day. Claude Code. Obsidian. The whole rig.

### 12 · CREDO · 02:45

Bigger picture, the why behind the what: engineering-led security beats compliance theater, every time. Techno-optimist by orientation. Building beats critiquing. Abundance is a posture. AI is leverage, not threat. What I read now is product strategy and acceleration — not pentests.

### 13 · HOME · 03:00

Off-screen: Warsaw, ten years, citizenship close. Wife, daughter twelve, son nine. They speak better Polish than I do and remind me daily. The kids have lived in three languages since birth — which means I lose every argument in all of them.

### 14 · THE ASK · 03:15

What I want from this offsite: a real conversation with someone in Finance Eng or IT Ops whose name I only know from incident channels. Steal one good idea from a function that isn't mine. Remember why I like the back-office crowd more than any flashy product team.

### 15 · CLOSE · 03:30

That's the file. Ten years in Warsaw. Onwards and upwards — only advance. If any beat landed — the Pizza Hut hack, Bugcrowd, DC4822, the Amazon disease, the PayPal-mafia school, the weirdos clause — come find me. I'm cheap. Coffee or a good incident story. Thank you.
